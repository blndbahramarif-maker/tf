Services we offer

Services we offer

Everything a small garage does for routine work and common failures, brought to your address in a fully stocked van. Tell us the make, model and the symptoms and you will get a clear price before anything is touched.

Servicing

Keep your vehicle running smoothly and efficiently. Interim and full services with oil, filters and a full check-over, recorded for your service history.

Brakes

Safe stopping power when you need it most. Pads, discs, calipers, shoes and fluid changes — inspected first, then replaced with quality parts.

Enquire

Timing belts

Prevent costly engine damage with timely replacement. Belts, tensioners and water pumps changed to the manufacturer's schedule and torque settings.

Enquire

Clutches

Smooth gear changes and better performance. Slipping, dragging or juddering clutches diagnosed and replaced — kits, bearings and flywheels where needed.

Enquire

Diagnostics

Accurate fault finding and expert advice. Warning lights read with professional equipment, then explained in plain English with the options and the cost.

General repairs

03 — How it works

Three steps, no garage visit

Booking a mobile mechanic should take one phone call. Here is exactly what happens.

Call or WhatsApp

Tell us the make, model, mileage and what the vehicle is doing. Photos and videos over WhatsApp help. You get a clear price or a diagnostic plan there and then.

We come to you

We arrive at your home, your workplace or the roadside with the tools and the parts for the job. You carry on with your day.

Job done & explained

The work is completed on site, the old parts are there to see and anything extra is agreed with you before it is fitted. Never a surprise on the bill.

Where we cover

Based in Rochester, Kent and working across the Medway towns and the surrounding area. If you are a little further out, call and ask — travel is usually not a problem.
