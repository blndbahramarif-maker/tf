About

Experienced. Professional. Mobile.

My name is Harem M S B and I am a professional mobile mechanic with 8 years of experience. I provide reliable vehicle repairs, servicing and diagnostics, bringing the service directly to you.

The mobile service is designed to make getting a mechanic easier and more convenient. Instead of booking your vehicle into a garage, arranging a lift and waiting for the work to be completed, I come to your home or workplace and carry out the work where your vehicle is. It saves you time, removes the hassle of recovery or drop-offs, and keeps your day moving while your vehicle is looked after.
