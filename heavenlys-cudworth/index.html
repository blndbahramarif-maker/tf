Parking & getting here

There is plenty of free parking around the salon. You can park:

  • Behind the salon
  • Along the main road
  • In the space directly in front of the shop windows

If you do struggle to find a space, you can park at the Barnsley Road & Bank Street car park. Once parked, look for the small entrance/ginnel that runs through towards the salon. This shortcut brings you straight out at the side of the salon.

Coming by public transport? There are several convenient bus routes from Barnsley.
